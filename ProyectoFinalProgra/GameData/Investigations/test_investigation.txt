INVESTIGATION REPORT 001

Case: The Hollow Room Murder
Classification: Homicide
Status: Under Investigation
Date: November 14, 2026

Incident Summary

At approximately 22:40, police received an emergency call from a neighbor reporting suspicious noises coming from apartment 304 at 17 Blackwood Street.

Upon arrival, officers discovered the body of Daniel Whitmore, aged 42, inside his living room. The victim had suffered a fatal injury. There were no obvious signs of forced entry.

Initial Findings

The apartment door was locked when officers arrived.

A broken glass was found near the living room sofa.

The victim's phone was missing.

A cup of coffee, partially consumed, was found on the kitchen counter.

The apartment appeared unusually tidy, except for a displaced chair near the entrance.

Persons of Interest

1. Margaret Whitmore — Wife

Claims she was visiting her sister during the estimated time of death. Her statement has not yet been independently verified.

2. Thomas Reed — Neighbor

Reported hearing an argument around 21:50. Claims he recognized two distinct voices but could not identify the second person.

3. Vincent Hale — Business Partner

Had a documented disagreement with the victim regarding missing company funds. Claims he had not spoken to Daniel in several days.

Evidence Collected

Evidence A: Broken drinking glass.

Evidence B: Coffee cup recovered from the kitchen.

Evidence C: Security camera footage from the building entrance.

Evidence D: Handwritten note found inside the victim's desk.

Preliminary Conclusion

The circumstances suggest that the victim may have known the perpetrator, given the absence of forced entry. However, this remains an unconfirmed hypothesis.

Further interviews and forensic analysis are required before establishing a definitive sequence of events.

Lead Investigator: Detective Arthur Collins
Department: Criminal Investigation Division
